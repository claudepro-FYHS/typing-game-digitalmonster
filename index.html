Why do I need a nickname?
The leaderboard can be seen by everyone, so it shows only your nickname — never your real name. Your real name, class and seat number are sent only to your teacher.
Rules: 2–12 letters, numbers or Chinese characters (no spaces). Rude or offensive words in any language are not allowed, and your teacher can change a nickname at any time.

BATTLE SETUP

How to play
  • Type the word on a virus monster to lock on and attack. Finish the word to beat it.
  • Viruses that reach you cost 1 ♥. Lose all ♥ and the battle ends.
  • Backspace releases your lock. Esc pauses.
  • Beaten viruses may drop items: 1 💣 clear screen, 2 ❄️ freeze 5 s, 3 🛡️ shield.
  • Boss: type its word to damage it. Type the words on its attack orbs to stop them!
  • Many partners have a special move: type words in a row to charge it, then press Space to evolve and attack.
  • Keep a combo of 25 words and your partner evolves too!
  • Multiplayer: anyone can attack any virus — whoever finishes the word first gets the points!
